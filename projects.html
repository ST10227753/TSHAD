Our Projects
& Initiatives

Sustainable income, skills development, community support, and environmental innovation — all powered by our members.

Income-Generating

Sustainable & Economic Projects

Recycling
Recycling Initiative
Collection and processing of paper, boxes, plastic, and a food waste to manure project.
Retail
TSHAD Tuckshop
Sells snacks and beverages, generating revenue to cover operational costs and fund programmes.
Printing
TSHAD Printing Shop
Embroidery, printing (screen/digital), and sewing services for customized apparel and promotional items.
Skills & Empowerment

Training & Development

Education
Braille for the Blind
Classes teach spelling, grammar, punctuation, and text formatting to visually impaired members.
Communication
Sign Language Classes
Classes provided to staff and volunteers to ensure effective communication with deaf individuals.
Advocacy
Autism Classes
Advocates for individuals with autism, providing education, support, resources, and promoting inclusive communities.

Community & Social

Support & Health Programmes

Mobility
Wheelchair Repairs
Services and loaning of mobility aids (wheelchairs/crutches) to members and community members.
Sports
TSHAD Sports Academy
Inclusive sports and fitness programmes like aerobics, karate, and table tennis to promote health and confidence.
Arts
TSHAD Theatre & Arts
Creates an inclusive cultural environment through acting, music, and fine arts for all members.
Environmental Innovation

Waste-to-Resource Projects

Energy
Waste-Based Ethanol
Converting waste materials into ethanol for sustainable household cooking, reducing reliance on paraffin.
Biodiesel
Used Cooking Oil to Biodiesel
Recycling cooking oil into bio-oil, a renewable alternative to fossil fuels for community use.
Composting
Food Waste into Compost
Converting food scraps into nutrient-rich compost to enrich soil and enhance local food production.
