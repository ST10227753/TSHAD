Mission & Vision

What Drives Us

Our Full Vision

01

To envision a future where individuals with disabilities are socially integrated, economically active, and financially self-sustainable. We strive to break down barriers and provide opportunities for social interaction, economic empowerment, and financial stability through innovative programmes.

Our Full Mission

02

To identify and enhance socio-economic livelihood opportunities for people with disabilities; actively engage in activities through the creation of viable and sustainable income-generating projects; and promote programs that advance social integration and mainstream people with disabilities.

How We Work

Inclusion in Practice

We work towards promoting inclusivity, empowerment, and self-sustainability. We strive to break down barriers and provide opportunities for social interaction, economic empowerment, and financial stability through innovative programmes.

Our efforts align with the Agenda 2030 for Sustainable Development, emphasising inclusion in all sectors — ensuring that no one is marginalized or left behind.

From recycling to Braille education, from wheelchair repairs to the arts, every programme we run is designed to affirm dignity and create real, lasting opportunity.

The Foundation of Inclusion

A Legacy Built on Compassion

Disability inclusion is essential for upholding human rights, sustainable development, peace, and security. These values were embodied by our founder, Mr. Manas Buswane Malinga, long before South Africa entered its democratic era.

His vision for empowerment was grounded in compassion, determination, and community spirit.

Mr. Manas Buswane Malinga ⋅ Founder, TSHAD 1990

At TSHAD, inclusion means creating real opportunities — spaces where people of all abilities can thrive, contribute, and be valued.

By focusing on accessible education, sustainable livelihoods, and meaningful participation, we continue to build upon the legacy of Mr. Malinga. His work reminds us that when we remove barriers and embrace diversity, we strengthen society as a whole.
