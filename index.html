Who We Are

T-SHAD

The Tembisa Self Help Association of the Disabled (TSHAD) is a community-based non-profit organisation with cross-disability representation, established in 1990 by Mr. Manas Buswane Malinga.

We envision a future where individuals with disabilities are socially integrated, economically active, and financially self-sustainable.

Empowering people with disabilities is about celebrating their strengths and unique perspectives.

Vision Forge

Where dreams take shape — creating unforgettable opportunities for the disabled community with passion, determination, and community spirit.

01

Vision Snapshot

To envision a future where individuals with disabilities are socially integrated, economically active, and financially self-sustainable. We strive to break barriers and provide opportunities for social interaction, economic empowerment, and financial stability.

02

Mission Snapshot

To identify and enhance socio-economic livelihood opportunities for people with disabilities; actively engage in activities through the creation of viable and sustainable income-generating projects; and promote programmes that advance social integration.
